A few helpful answers

How do I make a move?

Connect three or more matching neighboring tiles, including diagonals, then release. Swords attack, shields build armor, potions heal, runes charge your ability, and coins buy camp supplies. Every chain advances the enemy’s countdown. The in-game Guide explains the details; replay the prologue from Settings whenever you like.

Where is my progress saved?

Your journey is saved on your device after each turn. The current release does not include cloud saves or cross-device syncing. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app as a first troubleshooting step: doing so can remove your local progress.

What are coins and embers for?

Spend coins during an expedition on camp supplies and relic scouting. You earn embers from expedition progress and leftover coins when a run ends. Spend those embers at the Forge on lasting improvements for your next expedition.

How do I unlock Endless?

Defeat the Hollow King, the Chapter I boss. Endless and all five current campaign chapters are free. In Endless, you can carry up to five relics and swap them as you discover new ones.

How do purchases and restoration work?

Any available in-app purchases are processed by Apple. Use Restore purchases in the Shop with the same Apple Account used to buy them. An unavailable collection cannot be purchased. For a refund request, visit Apple’s Report a Problem service.

Can I report a bug or suggest an idea?

Absolutely. Use the support email above. If you are testing through TestFlight, you can also send feedback through TestFlight.
