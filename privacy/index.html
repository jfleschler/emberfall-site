A note on privacy

Your adventure stays yours.

Privacy policy · Last updated September 9, 2026

Emberfall: Hollow Woods is provided by Jonathan Fleschler. This policy covers the current game, this support website, and support correspondence.

Information collected by the game

The developer does not collect personal information or gameplay analytics through Emberfall. The game has no advertising, tracking, developer account system, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Your progress and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. The current release does not include Game Center or a cloud-save service.

Apple services and purchases

The game uses Apple’s StoreKit framework for purchase and restore functionality. When purchases are available, Apple processes them. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple’s services, including App Store and TestFlight distribution, are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, troubleshoot the issue, and improve the game. Support email is handled through Gmail, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

We keep correspondence as needed to resolve and refer back to support requests. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by emailing us; some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure its service, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or contact forms to this website. Fonts and artwork are served with the site. Following an external link or sending email takes you to that service’s own privacy practices.

Removing local progress

Deleting the app removes its local data from that installation. Device backups are managed through Apple’s device and backup settings. Because we do not receive your saved game, we cannot recover or delete it remotely.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our data practices change, including if we introduce Game Center, cloud saves, or other online features. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Jonathan Fleschler at jon.fleschler@gmail.com.
